NIF/NIE are the official tax identifiers that make invoices legally valid and traceable in Spain
Seller NIF → identifies
who issued the invoice

Seller NIF (Número de Identificación Fiscal)
What it is:
The
tax ID of the company or person issuing the invoice (the seller).
What it provides:
Uniquely identifies the supplier for tax purposes
Ensures the invoice can be traced back to the correct business
Used by authorities (AEAT) to validate and track transactions
Where it appears:
Mandatory invoice field (e.g. BT‑32)
Buyer NIF (for businesses)
What it is:
The
tax ID of the customer when the customer is a Spanish business entity.
What it provides:
Identifies the business receiving the invoice
Enables correct tax reporting and matching on both sides of the transaction
Buyer NIE (Número de Identidad de Extranjero)
What it is:
A
tax identification number for foreign individuals
(non-Spanish nationals) in Spain.
When it is used:
If the buyer is an
individual (not a company)
and is a foreign resident or taxpayer
Used instead of NIF for those individuals

Seller NIF (Business or Individual)
✅ What to validate
From your source document, the Seller NIF is a
mandatory invoice field
(BT‑32).
In practice, NIF can represent:
Companies (legal entities)
Spanish individuals (self-employed)
🔹 Standard NIF Formats
A.
Companies (Legal Entities)
Format:
[A-Z][0-9]{7}[A-Z0-9]
Examples:
B12345678
A98765432
F1234567J
Breakdown:
1st character = entity type (letter)
Next 7 digits = unique identifier
Last character = control/check character
B.
Spanish Individuals (DNI-based NIF)
Format:
[0-9]{8}[A-Z]
Example:
12345678Z
Breakdown:
8 digits = national ID number
1 letter = checksum (derived from number)
✅ Key Validation Rules (NIF)
Must match one of the valid patterns above
Must be
uppercase
Must not contain spaces or symbols
Length:
9 characters standard
Optional (advanced):
Verify checksum letter (for individuals and some entities)
Buyer NIF / NIE
From your document, the buyer field accepts
NIF or NIE
(BT‑48).
🔹 A. Buyer NIF (Business or Spanish Individual)
Same rules as above apply.
🔹 B. NIE (Foreign Individual ID)
✅ Format
[X|Y|Z][0-9]{7}[A-Z]
Examples:
X1234567L
Y7654321P
Z1234567R
🔍 Breakdown
Part
Meaning
X / Y / Z
Prefix identifying foreign nationals
7 digits
Unique identifier
Final letter
Checksum
✅ NIE Validation Rules
Must start with:
X, Y, or Z
Must be exactly 9 characters
Last character must be a letter
Must be uppercase
Optional advanced validation:
Convert prefix to number (X=0, Y=1, Z=2)
Then apply standard DNI checksum logic
Practical API Validation Logic
✅ Recommended Validation Order
IF matches company NIF pattern → valid NIF
ELSE IF matches individual NIF pattern → valid NIF
ELSE IF matches NIE pattern → valid NIE
ELSE → invalid identifier
✅ Regex Examples (Production-Ready)
You can use these directly in your backend:
