Main Flow
Overview
Sage ERP submits the NIF and onboarding profile, records the 
activation scope, secures the signing certificate, then configures the 
selected private e-invoice platform before registration is confirmed.
VALIDATION LOOP
422 Path
Why It Loops
A rejected NIF or incomplete profile returns to identity ingestion before 
certificate storage or AEAT activation begins, keeping correction close 
to the source data.
RETRY MODELING
Retry Modeling
Why The Loop Stays
A transient AEAT or network timeout is placed in a queued backoff 
flow, then re-enters activation without repeating completed validation 
and certificate work.
