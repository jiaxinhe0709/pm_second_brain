Engineering & Implementation Suggestions - DRAFT 
To accelerate development and minimize the risk of unhandled edge cases in the mathematical 
validation layer, the engineering team should adhere to the following guidelines:
Do Not Reinvent the Math


===== PAGE 4 =====

The C# team should avoid writing custom Modulo 11 and Modulo 23 algorithms from scratch. Spanish 
corporate identifiers (CIFs) contain highly specific edge cases regarding foreign entities and partnerships 
that are notoriously difficult to maintain.
Instead, leverage established open-source NuGet packages that specialize in Spanish tax identification 
logic. Suggested libraries to evaluate include:
: A highly optimized .NET library 
NIF.DNI.NIE.CIF.ValidationNIF.DNI.NIE.CIF.Validation
specifically built for validating Spanish identifiers, supporting both synchronous and 
asynchronous batch processing.
: A broader .NET library that handles tax ID validations 
CountryValidatorCountryValidator
(including Spain's NIF/CIF).

create an internal C# interface (e.g.,
