# Receiving questions and decisions

This log records the 20 November requirements to prevent duplicate ERP invoices and save source files before conversion. It also records the 23 November original/copy research and clarification. ERP means the customer's business software. [SPEC026](SPEC-SEI-026-invoice-retrieval-logical-feature-specification.md#copy-only-engineering-behaviour) owns the required behaviour; [SPEC027](SPEC-SEI-027-invoice-retrieval-sequence-and-reuse-assessment.md#4-open-engineering-decisions) owns engineering qualification. Evidence, product decisions and open questions keep separate states below.
