# Sending questions and decisions

These questions come from the sending notes supplied on 20 November 2026. ERP means the customer's business software. SPFE is Spain's public e-invoicing platform. B2B means business to business. Selecting several invoices in the ERP, sending a request to Sage, and sending an invoice package to SPFE are separate steps. Ten invoices is an example. We have not agreed that grouping is mandatory or that a new Sage API is needed. We have not confirmed whether this is comparable with France e-reporting.

The table is the source for these questions. [SPEC024 SND-G01–09](SPEC-SEI-024-invoice-sending-sequence-and-reuse-assessment.md#4-open-engineering-decisions) lists the technical checks still needed. The owner column suggests who to ask; those teams have not accepted an assignment. The [shared glossary](../../context/GLO-SEI-001-e-invoicing-terminology.md) explains other terms. Recording an input does not approve a product rule, service responsibility or delivery date.

Show which client and certificate the work belongs to, and what happens after a failure. Decide service responsibilities before assigning new components.
