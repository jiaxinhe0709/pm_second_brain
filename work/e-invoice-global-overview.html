How Sage plans to support e-invoicing across countries

Customers would work in their Sage product. A shared service would check the country's rules, uses a suitable way to send or receive the invoice, and returns a clear result.

In Sage productsCustomers would set up their business, create or review invoices, and see progress.
In Sage's shared serviceSage would check country rules, prepare invoices, choose connections, and keep results together.
Outside SageCountry directories, invoice networks, providers and tax authorities return their own results. Delivery, acceptance and payment are different outcomes.
