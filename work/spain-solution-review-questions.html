Spain e-invoicing: solution review questions

Ask these in order: first agree the scope and owners, then follow the customer journeys, and finish with release readiness.

These questions organise decisions already recorded in the 13 draft specs. They do not replace the detailed decision registers. For each answer, record the decision, the person responsible, supporting evidence, remaining work and target date. An unresolved answer remains open.
