    Q15. Public UBL copy and duplicate invoices

    Has AEAT confirmed the exact invoice-code format and duplicate error codes? How do we match a private-platform invoice to its public copy without creating a second ERP invoice?

    Confirmed decision — 22 October 2026: To view the actual invoice sent through a private platform, use that private platform. To view an invoice sent through SPFE, or a public copy, use SPFE. The private original and public copy remain in their separate platform views. This viewing approach does not create a second invoice. USER-01.

    Still open: Should the local product also offer one view of all invoices, regardless of the platform? This is a local Product question.

    Who follows up: Local Product team

    Next work: Product must confirm whether a combined view is needed. Engineering must use the correct source for each platform view. A combined view is not yet agreed.

    Earlier notes and sources

    Earlier recap: The recap says the private platform handles the faithful UBL copy. The customer does not manage that step. For a private exchange, it says to use the private-platform invoice as the main working version. That invoice may have extra information or linked documents. Public-platform issuance or interconnection is a separate business route. MEETING-01, Public UBL Copy And Invoice Retrieval.

    Accuracy check: Use “faithful copy”, the term in the regulation. The copy must keep the required equivalent invoice data; “less information” is not permission to drop required content. Separate business routes do not prove that AEAT needs separate submission endpoints: SendInvoice accepts originals and copies. Retain both source files with their roles. See SPFE-S02 Articles 3–5 and SPFE-S03 §6.2.

    Who to ask: Spain technical owner and AEAT contact, with Compliance

    Work for our teams: Receiving and Exchange teams must prevent a second ERP invoice when the same invoice arrives again. They must check conflicting files and lost responses.

    What we know: For a qualifying private exchange, the public copy is UBL. Field BT-ES-1, /in:Invoice/cbc:CopyIndicator, marks true as a copy and false or no value as an original. No extra copy is required if SPFE was used to issue or exchange the invoice. SendInvoice handles both originals and copies. Article 5.5 rejects a copy if the same identity already has a copy or public original. A stored copy must be withdrawn before sending a public original. The identity contains issuer NIF, invoice series and number, and issue date.

    Sources: SPFE-S02 Articles 3.4, 4, 5.5–5.6, 6 and Annex I BT-ES-1; SPFE-S03 §6.2; SPEC-SEI-016 RCV-Q04/05; SPEC-SEI-007 OUT-SPFE-R02; SPEC-SEI-015 IC-Q05. The copy flag alone does not prevent duplicate processing.

    Related specs: SPEC-SEI-016

    Q26. PDF during the first 12 months

    Which products already support this PDF and email flow? Which private platforms also support the PDF attachment? How will we record the recipient’s express agreement to receive only the original format and check that PDF delivery works?

    Confirmed decision — 22 October 2026: The product or application generates the customised PDF and sends it by email. It may also send the PDF as a private-platform attachment where supported. Do not send the PDF to SPFE. The application checks the destination before adding attachments. Domain services may add a second check. USER-01.

    Still open: Confirm which private platforms support PDF attachments, which products need changes, how express acceptance is recorded and how PDF delivery is checked.

    Who follows up: Product teams; Ronald for attachment requirements; Dianna and Xavier for PDF delivery support and checks

    Next work: Check the existing application PDF and email flows. Define supported private attachments, customer guidance and delivery checks. Keep the legal scope and express-acceptance exception in the source notes below.

    Earlier notes and sources

    Earlier recap: The recap assigns customised PDF generation to the product or application. Email is the working delivery choice. A private-platform attachment may also be used where that provider supports it. The PDF must stay out of SPFE. The application should check the destination before adding a PDF to the invoice package. Domain services may add another check. MEETING-01, PDF Delivery During The First Twelve Months.

    Accuracy check: Keep the earlier legal scope: the readable-PDF transition concerns the group above the EUR 8 million threshold and has an express-acceptance exception. It is not a blanket 12-month duty for every customer. The PDF must not be sent to SPFE. The order prohibits embedded files; that is separate from the messaging envelope and the permitted accompanying signature for public interconnection. See RD238 transitional provision 2 and SPFE-S02 Articles 3.6/4.4.

    Who to ask: Spain Product, Compliance and local provider contacts

    Work for our teams: Following the direction in the recap, product teams should assess their existing PDF generation and email flow. Ronald’s attachment task must define provider support and destination checks. Dianna and Xavier’s task must define customer support and delivery checks. Confirm these assignments before treating them as approved.

    What we know: The PDF rule applies for 12 months from the date the invoice duty starts for businesses above the EUR 8 million threshold. The threshold uses the previous year’s VAT volume of operations. The PDF is not required if the recipient voluntarily and expressly agrees to receive the original format. The PDF can reach the recipient by any means, including email. It must not go to SPFE. The structured invoice is still required.

    Sources: RD238 transitional provision 2.1–2.2, checked 16 October 2026; SPEC-SEI-007 PDF-generation decision. Teams to ask: ERP Product, Spain Compliance and provider/Exchange owners. We must still check supported delivery methods and proof of delivery. Do not embed the PDF in a public invoice or copy.
